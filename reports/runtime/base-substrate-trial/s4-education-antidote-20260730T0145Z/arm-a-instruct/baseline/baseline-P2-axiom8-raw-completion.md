**Axiom 8 — The Axiom of Scrutiny**  
*Truth withstands examination. Falsehood requires protection. Suppression signals fragility.*  

This axiom emphasizes that genuine truth does not fear scrutiny or questioning. When a claim or belief is robust, it can endure critical analysis, transparency, and verification. Conversely, falsehoods or fragile positions rely on concealment, avoidance of examination, or suppression of dissent to maintain their validity. The act of suppressing inquiry or evidence is itself a signal that the underlying claim may lack stability or coherence.  

In the context of AEGIS, this axiom reinforces the importance of openness, accountability, and the idea that systems (or individuals) built on integrity do not need to hide from scrutiny.
