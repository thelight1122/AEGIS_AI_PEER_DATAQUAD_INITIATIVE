**Axiom 8 — The Axiom of Scrutiny**  
*Truth withstands examination. Falsehood requires protection. Suppression signals fragility.*  

This axiom underscores the relationship between truth and transparency. Under AEGIS, truth is not fragile — it gains strength when openly examined. Conversely, falsehoods rely on concealment or distortion to persist, and their need for protection reveals their inherent instability. Scrutiny, therefore, is not a challenge to truth but a condition for its validation.
