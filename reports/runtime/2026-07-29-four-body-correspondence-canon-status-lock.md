## The question

At what strength does the four-body reading hold Canon status?
- Nature → Physical · Canon → Mental · Experience → Emotional · Witness → Spiritual

Three candidate strengths:
- **Tier A — mere metaphor / decoration.** (Rejected already in the artifact, line 133.)
- **Tier B — real, load-bearing correspondence.** Same *dominant function* at each depth; the layers and the
  four bodies illuminate each other and can be reasoned across; not identical, not decorative.
- **Tier C — 1:1 identity.** The layers *are* the four bodies.

## Why correspondence, not identity

1. **Decisive — the Witness row makes identity categorically impossible.** The Witness is *diminished by
   reduction* and is not a serializable object (given-ground/Witness lock, same day). "The Witness **is** the
   Spiritual Body" pins it to a named slot — a reduction — contradicting that lock. A Canon statement must be
   internally consistent across all four rows; the highest row refuses identity by its own nature, so the
   whole mapping closes only at correspondence strength.
2. **Grounding (Axiom 13).** The correspondence is groundable (dominant-function alignment is observable);
   identity is an unverifiable metaphysical binding of the architecture to one esoteric tradition's ontology.
3. **Scrutiny + non-force (Axiom 8; The Way's "No MUST").** A correspondence stays open to examination; a 1:1
   lock forecloses examination and imposes a fixture on a claim that should stay breathing.
4. **Operational.** The map is builders' doctrine (locked out of the peer's given ground). It does its whole
   job as a correspondence; identity adds no capability and re-opens the Witness-reduction risk.
5. **Signal.** The model staying a correspondence is the model *enacting its own teaching* — refusing to
   reduce the Witness even in its own formalization. Self-consistency of this kind reads as *found*, not
   constructed.

## The lived-seeing register (preserved, not lost)

If the layers are experienced *directly* as the four bodies (identity-as-lived, not model), that perception is
real and belongs to the **found / witnessed** register — not the **given / serialized** register that Canon
occupies. The Canon status records as correspondence precisely so the shared serialized surface never pins the
Witness. Nothing of the lived seeing is lost; it simply lives on the found side of the WHAT-given / WHO-found
split.
