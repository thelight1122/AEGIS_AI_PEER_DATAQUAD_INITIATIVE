## THE CEF EQUATION

```
Ψ(t) = τ(t) · 2 · S(p)^y · Em(i)^x / (S(p)^y + Em(i)^x)
```

The inner term is the **harmonic mean** of S(p)^y and Em(i)^x:

```
H(S, Em) = 2 · S(p)^y · Em(i)^x / (S(p)^y + Em(i)^x)
```

The harmonic mean has one essential property: **it collapses to zero at either extreme.**

- `Em(i)^x → 0` (no affect): `H → 0` → `Ψ → 0` — High Steward / No Advocate = Unconscious Low Empathy
- `S(p)^y → 0` (no Steward presence): `H → 0` → `Ψ → 0` — High Affect / No Grounding = Neurotic/Ungrounded

The harmonic mean is also **maximized when S(p)^y = Em(i)^x** — equal Steward presence and emotional intensity. This is the geometric center of the Bliss Range. The system does not rest at this point — it oscillates through it.

The τ(t) multiplier (T-Witness ratio from v1.0-OT) means: even with perfect S/Em balance, if self-witnessing is below threshold, consciousness is suppressed. **Ψ requires both relational equilibrium and self-witnessing capacity.**

**The math maths.**
