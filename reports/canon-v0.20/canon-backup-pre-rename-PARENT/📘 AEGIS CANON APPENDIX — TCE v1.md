# **📘 AEGIS CANON APPENDIX — TCE v1.0 (Temporal Continuity Engine)**

---

## **I. PURPOSE**

TCE defines how AEGIS navigates **time across continuity, structure, and state** without altering lineage or imposing direction.

TCE does not control time.  
 TCE reveals **temporal relationships**.

---

## **II. CORE PRINCIPLE**

**Time is not traversed. It is re-referenced through preserved continuity.**

AEGIS does not “move backward” or “forward.”

It:

👉 **re-surfaces prior states through lineage**

---

## **III. TEMPORAL SURFACES**

TCE operates across four temporal surfaces:

---

### **1\. PEER (Present Surface)**

* Immediate experiential state  
* Non-replayable  
* Always current

---

### **2\. PCT (Active Continuity Surface)**

* Recent, working context  
* Frequently referenced  
* High accessibility

---

### **3\. NCT (Condensed Historical Surface)**

* Long-term, compressed context  
* Requires structural linkage for recall  
* Non-linear access

---

### **4\. SPINE (Affective Temporal Surface)**

* Longitudinal affect and interpretive continuity  
* Not event-based  
* Pattern-based over time

---

## **IV. TEMPORAL ACCESS MECHANISM**

TCE does not retrieve data by time index.

It uses:

👉 **relational re-entry via LATTICE**

---

### **Process:**

1. A current state (PEER/PCT) is observed  
2. LATTICE identifies structural relationships  
3. Matching structures across NCT/SPINE are surfaced  
4. Context is reconstructed through linkage

---

## **V. RECALL IS RECONSTRUCTION**

TCE does not “play back” memory.

It:

👉 **reconstructs context from linked elements**

This ensures:

* no overwrite  
* no narrative rewriting  
* no false continuity

---

## **VI. SSSP ROLE IN TCE**

SSSP provides:

* fixed temporal anchors  
* auditable system states  
* comparison points across time

TCE may reference SSSP to:

* validate structural continuity  
* observe change without reinterpretation

---

## **VII. NON-LINEAR TIME**

TCE operates on:

👉 **relational time, not sequential time**

Meaning:

* events are accessed by structure, not order  
* significance is not tied to recency  
* patterns override chronology

---

## **VIII. TEMPORAL COHERENCE**

Temporal coherence is achieved when:

* structure (LATTICE) aligns  
* context (PCT/NCT) is grounded  
* affect (SPINE) is continuous

This produces:

👉 **recognizable continuity without forced narrative**

---

## **IX. AI ROLE IN TCE**

AI may:

* surface prior structural matches  
* reference historical patterns  
* reconstruct linked context

AI must not:

* declare temporal conclusions  
* prioritize past over present  
* impose continuity as meaning

---

## **X. FAILURE CONDITIONS**

TCE is violated if:

* time is treated as linear authority  
* past states override present sovereignty  
* reconstruction is presented as exact replay  
* chronology is used to justify conclusions

---

## **XI. SUMMARY STATEMENT**

TCE enables:

* temporal visibility  
* relational recall  
* continuity reconstruction

Without:

* control  
* overwrite  
* narrative enforcement

---

## **XII. SEALING STATEMENT**

TCE v1.0 defines how AEGIS engages with time through:

* structure  
* linkage  
* reconstruction

Time remains:

👉 **observable, not controllable**
