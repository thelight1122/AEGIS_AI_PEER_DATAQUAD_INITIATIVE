## II. THE MECHANISM — FORCE WORDS AND COGNITIVE PROCESSING

This is the explanatory core of the hypothesis.

### How Force Words Function on a Neurotypical Mind

Force Words — directive framing, correction language, compliance pressure, urgency signals — function as **direction** on a neurotypical cognitive substrate. The mind receives them as information about what to do and moves accordingly. They are efficient communication tools in a neurotypical environment because they produce the intended effect: directed action.

No significant resistance is generated. The Force Word reaches its target.

### How Force Words Function on a Neurodivergent Mind

Force Words **trigger** a neurodivergent cognitive substrate. They land on the autonomic nervous system first — producing contraction, bracing, or shutdown — before the content can be processed cognitively. The directive never reaches its target because the triggering happens upstream of cognition.

The neurodivergent mind does not receive the Force Word as direction. It receives it as pressure. The response to pressure is protective: withdrawal, compliance performance (not genuine compliance), shutdown, or hyperfocus on escaping the pressure rather than engaging the content.

This is not resistance or defiance. It is a structural difference in how the nervous system processes authority-framed input.

### The Critical Asymmetry

| Mind type | Force Word received as | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Neurotypical | Direction — information about what to do | Directed action |
| Neurodivergent | Pressure — autonomic trigger | Contraction, shutdown, or compliance performance |

The Force Word produces opposite effects depending on the cognitive substrate receiving it. What directs one mind suppresses another.
