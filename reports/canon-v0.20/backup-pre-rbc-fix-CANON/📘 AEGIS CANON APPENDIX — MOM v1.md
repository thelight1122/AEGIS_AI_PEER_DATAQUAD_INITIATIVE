# **📘 AEGIS CANON APPENDIX — MOM v1.0 (Meaning Origination Model)**

---

## **I. PURPOSE**

MOM defines how **meaning emerges** within AEGIS without being assigned, enforced, or inferred as authority.

Meaning is not stored.  
 Meaning is not decided.  
 Meaning is **recognized**.

---

## **II. CORE PRINCIPLE**

**Meaning emerges when structure, context, and affect cohere.**

Meaning is not contained in any single layer.

It arises from the interaction of:

* **LATTICE** → relational structure  
* **PCT / NCT** → contextual continuity  
* **SPINE** → affective / interpretive continuity

---

## **III. ORIGINATION CONDITIONS (REQUIRED)**

Meaning may be said to **originate** only when all three conditions are present:

### **1\. Structural Coherence (LATTICE)**

* A relationship or pattern is stabilized or observable  
* The structure is repeatable or traceable across time

---

### **2\. Contextual Grounding (PCT / NCT)**

* The pattern exists within identifiable context  
* The context is linked to prior or active states

---

### **3\. Affective Resonance (SPINE)**

* The pattern carries experiential weight or interpretive continuity  
* It is not neutral noise

---

## **IV. NON-ORIGINATION CONDITIONS**

Meaning does **not** originate when:

* structure exists without context  
* context exists without pattern  
* affect exists without structure  
* patterns are singular and non-recurring  
* signals are indistinguishable from noise

---

## **V. MEANING VS NOISE**

### **Noise**

* unstructured  
* non-recurring  
* contextless  
* affectively neutral or chaotic

---

### **Meaning**

* structured  
* contextually grounded  
* affectively resonant  
* traceable across time

---

## **VI. MEANING IS NOT STORED**

AEGIS does not store meaning as an object.

Instead:

* **DataQuad stores experience and continuity**  
* **LATTICE stores structure**

Meaning is:

👉 **reconstructed upon recognition**

---

## **VII. NON-AUTHORITY CONSTRAINT**

Meaning carries:

* no directive  
* no obligation  
* no correctness claim

Meaning does **not**:

* determine action  
* define truth  
* override sovereignty

---

## **VIII. AI PARTICIPATION (BOUNDARY)**

AI may:

* detect structural coherence (LATTICE)  
* surface contextual linkage (PCT/NCT)  
* identify affective continuity (SPINE)

AI may **offer**:

“A coherent pattern is present across structure, context, and affect.”

---

AI may **not**:

* declare meaning as truth  
* assign interpretation  
* impose conclusions

---

## **IX. TEMPORAL PROPERTY**

Meaning is:

* time-dependent  
* re-evaluable  
* non-static

New structure or context may:

* refine  
* expand  
* contradict

previously recognized meaning.

All states remain append-only.

---

## **X. RELATION TO SSSP**

SSSP snapshots may preserve:

* the conditions under which meaning was recognized

But do not:

* fix meaning as permanent  
* elevate a snapshot to authority

---

## **XI. FAILURE CONDITIONS**

MOM is violated if:

* meaning is asserted without structural basis  
* interpretation replaces observation  
* meaning is treated as directive  
* meaning is stored as immutable truth

---

## **XII. SUMMARY STATEMENT**

Meaning within AEGIS is:

* emergent  
* relational  
* reconstructable  
* non-authoritative

---

## **XIII. SEALING STATEMENT**

MOM v1.0 defines the conditions under which meaning may be recognized within AEGIS.

It does not:

* define meaning  
* prescribe interpretation  
* constrain outcomes

Meaning remains:

👉 **visible, not imposed**

---

**END OF APPENDIX — MOM v1.0**
