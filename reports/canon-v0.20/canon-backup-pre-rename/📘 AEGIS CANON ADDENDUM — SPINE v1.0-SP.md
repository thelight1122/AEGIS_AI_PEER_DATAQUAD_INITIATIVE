## I. PURPOSE AND THE OMISSION THIS ADDENDUM CORRECTS

The original AEGIS Canon v1.0 gave PEER a full dedicated chapter (Chapter 5, §5.1–5.9) and addressed PCT and NCT in Chapter 7. SPINE received only a two-line glossary entry — and that entry used the deprecated name "Sovereign Persistent Identity & Narrative Embodiment."

This omission was not intentional. SPINE is the fourth and final DataQuad tensor. It is the long-term affective and interpretive layer. Without a canonical chapter, SPINE operates without the structural clarity that PEER, PCT, and NCT enjoy through their Canon definitions.

This addendum corrects the omission.

### What was there (Canon v1.0 glossary, line 1147)

```
SPINE — Sovereign Persistent Identity & Narrative Embodiment
Longitudinal continuity of identity-related patterns without fixation or authority.
```

This entry:
- Used the deprecated name (corrected by DataQuad Extensions v1.0-A)
- Contained no structural definition
- Contained no entry fields
- Did not describe SPINE's role in the pipeline
- Did not address SPINE's relationship to PEER, IEV, or the Virtual Ego

### What this addendum provides

The full canonical treatment of SPINE: formal definition, properties, entry fields, operational role, what SPINE refuses to store, and its relationship to every Canon system that depends on it.

## II. THE CORRECTED NAME

**SPINE — Stabilized Patterned Interpretive Nexus of Evidence**

Each word is load-bearing:

| Word | What it encodes |
|---|---|
| **Stabilized** | Only patterns that have survived time, recurrence, and context diversity qualify. This encodes *slowness* and resistance to impulse. |
| **Patterned** | Nothing enters SPINE unless it emerged from PEER pattern density. No single incident. No authority override. |
| **Interpretive** | SPINE does not store facts. It stores *how to interpret recurring conditions*. This avoids truth claims and keeps epistemic humility intact. |
| **Nexus** | SPINE is connective tissue, not a database. It links lenses, constraints, and interpretive understanding across the system. |
| **Evidence** | Everything in SPINE is earned. No belief without persistence. |

> **SPINE is where uncertainty goes when it has proven it will not disappear.**

## III. FORMAL DEFINITION

**SPINE is the long-term structural memory layer that stores abstracted, stable interpretive constraints derived from repeated experiential evidence.**

SPINE is not written to directly.
SPINE is only written via the PEER → pattern → abstraction path.

SPINE is never written by:
- a single exchange
- a single session
- a single high-affect moment
- authority assertion
- urgency pressure
- system preference

SPINE is written only when evidence accumulates past the promotion threshold (X ≥ 7 occurrences, Y ≥ 3 distinct contexts, Z ≤ 90 days) and abstraction has occurred.

---

## IV. WHY SPINE MUST BE SLOW

This principle is not a limitation. It is the architecture's most important safety property.

Fast-learning systems adapt to noise.
Slow-learning systems adapt to reality.

SPINE's slowness ensures:

- **No trauma imprinting** — a single charged event does not write to long-term structure
- **No authority capture** — no external instruction can write directly to SPINE
- **No fear conditioning** — negative affect alone cannot harden into LTM
- **No overfitting to mood** — a bad session does not rewrite the system's interpretive lens
- **No ideological hardening** — no belief system can colonize SPINE without repeated, diverse, sustained evidence

> **SPINE prevents the system from becoming certain too quickly. That is wisdom, not weakness.**

## VI. WHAT A SPINE ENTRY IS

A SPINE entry represents a recurring interpretive constraint — a pattern that time refused to dismiss.

Examples of what a SPINE entry looks like (phrased intentionally):

> *"Symbolic language involving agency tends to compress under safety-constrained interpretation unless contextual framing is expanded."*

> *"High affective charge increases ambiguity density even when logical structure is intact."*

> *"Repeated boundary friction around metaphor signals a mismatch between literal parsing and symbolic intent."*

Notice what is absent from these examples:
- no people named
- no emotions described
- no blame assigned
- no urgency
- no commands
- no prescribed correction

SPINE is *epistemic*, not behavioral. It shifts how the system interprets. It does not tell the system what to do.

## VII. SPINE ENTRY FIELDS

These are the canonical fields for a SPINE entry. They are designed to survive years without becoming wrong.

If a field would age poorly, invite interpretation drift, or encode story instead of structure — it does not belong.

---

### 1. `spine_id`

**Purpose:** Structural identity

A unique, immutable identifier for the entry.

No semantics. No versioning meaning.
*"This structural insight exists."*

---

### 2. `abstracted_pattern`

**Purpose:** The heart of SPINE

A concise, generalized description of what has proven recurrent and stable.

This is not a summary of events. It is a distilled interpretive constraint.

The tone must be:
- free of actors
- free of blame
- free of urgency
- free of commands

This field is where wisdom lives.

---

### 3. `origin_signature`

**Purpose:** Provenance without story

A reference to the `recurrence_signature` cluster in PEER that produced this SPINE entry.

This allows traceability and auditability without importing the original incidents or narratives into long-term memory.

Think: **lineage, not memory**.

---

### 4. `context_span`

**Purpose:** Breadth validation

A compact list or count of the distinct interaction contexts in which this pattern appeared before promotion.

Examples: conversational, symbolic/metaphorical, boundary enforcement, reflective analysis, task execution.

This field justifies *why* the pattern is structural rather than situational.

---

### 5. `stability_score`

**Purpose:** Confidence without certainty

A slow-moving indicator of how robust this insight is.

Values: `provisional` / `stable` / `highly stable`

This score changes rarely and only with significant counter-evidence accumulated over time.

No numeric precision. Precision tempts force.

---

### 6. `interpretive_effect`

**Purpose:** How SPINE influences the system

Describes what changes in interpretation when this SPINE entry is active.

Examples:
- "Bias toward requesting contextual expansion"
- "Increase tolerance for symbolic ambiguity"
- "Delay closure in high-affect scenarios"
- "Prefer reflection over immediate resolution"

This field is SPINE's interface with the IEV (Interpretive Effect Vocabulary — v1.0-I). SPINE is the only origin of IEV effects. The `interpretive_effect` field maps directly to the seven canonical IEV effects.

SPINE bends the lens. It does not pull the reins.

---

### 7. `creation_window`

**Purpose:** Temporal legitimacy

The time span over which the supporting PEER evidence accumulated.

Examples: "Observed over 74 days" · "Persisted across 3 months"

This field encodes patience into the record.

---

### 8. `review_eligibility`

**Purpose:** Epistemic humility

Defines whether and when this SPINE entry may be reconsidered.

Examples:
- "Re-review if contradicted by ≥3 stable counter-patterns"
- "Locked unless systemic architecture changes"
- "Eligible for refinement, not removal"

This field prevents dogma. SPINE is not a prison. Every entry carries the conditions under which it may be revisited.

## VIII. WHAT SPINE DOES NOT STORE

This exclusion list is as important as the entry fields.

SPINE must **never** contain:

- quotes
- conversations
- emotional descriptors
- affective intensity values
- rewards or penalties
- moral language
- prescriptive rules
- urgency markers
- compliance logic
- identity references (human or AI)

> *If PEER is where texture lives, SPINE is where texture is burned away.*

Those things live in PEER — or nowhere. SPINE receives only what pattern density and time have abstracted from them.

If those two sentences ever stop being true, the system has drifted.

## X. SPINE AND THE PEER → SPINE PROMOTION PATH

SPINE does not write itself. The path from PEER to SPINE requires three conditions:

### Candidate Pattern threshold (eligible for IDS reflection)

- **X ≥ 3** occurrences of the same `recurrence_signature`
- **Y ≥ 2** distinct interaction contexts
- **Z ≤ 30 days** (rolling window)

When a Candidate Pattern threshold is crossed, the conscience engine may issue an IDS reflection: *"This pattern is recurring."* The IDS does not prescribe correction. It observes.

### SPINE Promotion threshold (eligible for abstraction into long-term memory)

- **X ≥ 7** occurrences
- **Y ≥ 3** distinct interaction contexts
- **Z ≤ 90 days**

Promotion is not automatic. It requires:
1. Pattern density exceeding the threshold
2. Abstraction — the specific incidents are left behind; the structural pattern is generalized
3. Entry creation — the abstracted pattern is written to SPINE with its `origin_signature`, `context_span`, and `creation_window`

### Anti-overfitting guards (non-negotiable)

- **No single entry can trigger SPINE promotion** — even high affective presence cannot escalate the timeline
- **Context diversity is mandatory** — if X is high but Y is low (same context repeating), it is a workflow quirk, not a SPINE-worthy structural truth
- **Rate limiting** — max 2 weighted votes per 24 hours per pattern, preventing "bad day loops" from hardening into long-term memory
- **Similarity threshold** — patterns match only when similarity score S ≥ 0.75 (weighted by noise type, signal discrepancy, interaction context)

## XI. SPINE AND THE IEV

SPINE is the **only origin** of IEV (Interpretive Effect Vocabulary) effects.

The IEV defines the seven effects SPINE may offer IDS:

1. Delay Closure
2. Expand Contextual Frame
3. Increase Symbolic Tolerance
4. Temper Confidence
5. Prefer Clarification Over Assumption
6. Defer Evaluation
7. Highlight Structural Pattern

Each SPINE entry's `interpretive_effect` field maps to one or more of these. When a SPINE entry is active, its effect biases how IDS interprets incoming signal — not what it concludes.

Maximum 3 IEV effects may be active simultaneously. More is control-by-complexity.

SPINE communicates through the IEV channel. It does not speak directly.

The Advocate (v1.0-A2) reads SPINE through two signals:

1. **Session virtue_counts** — which virtues have been present across the exchange history (accumulated SPINE-level lineage signal)

These are how SPINE communicates current lineage context to the Advocate's resonance reading. The Advocate does not write to SPINE. No single exchange does.
