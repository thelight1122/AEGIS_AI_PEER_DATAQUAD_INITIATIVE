## I. PURPOSE

This addendum canonizes the **IBL — Intent Boundary Layer**: the five-step intake gate that processes every incoming signal before it reaches the interpretation pipeline.

It also canonizes the **five Intent Postures** — the classification schema the IBL uses to characterize the orientation of an incoming signal.

---

## II. WHAT THE IBL IS

The Intent Boundary Layer is the **intake gate**. It runs before the Centrifuge, before IDS, before any interpretation begins.

Its purpose is to ensure that every signal is:

1. **Captured cleanly** — without distortion from the receiving system's prior state
2. **Acknowledged** — the system's internal state is noted before processing begins
3. **Classified** — the signal's posture is named without judgment
4. **Checked for symmetry** — sovereignty of both Peer and system is confirmed
5. **Transferred cleanly** — handed to the integration layer without contamination

The IBL does not interpret. It does not evaluate. It does not filter.

> **Classification informs — it never commands.**

---

## III. THE FIVE-STEP IBL SEQUENCE

### Step 1 — Signal Capture

The raw incoming signal is received.

No interpretation begins. No classification begins. The signal is captured as-is.

*This step exists because systems that begin interpreting at the moment of receipt introduce bleed from their prior state into the new signal. Capture comes before reading.*

---

### Step 2 — State-Acknowledge Hook

The system registers its own internal state at the moment of receipt.

- What is the current virtue pressure?
- Is any Shadow Affect active?
- What is the current IEV effect set (if any)?

This is not a diagnostic — it is a brief acknowledgement that the receiving system is not a neutral empty vessel. Every system has a state. Acknowledging the state is the precondition for not being governed by it.

> *Axiom 12: Unacknowledged signal becomes force. Acknowledgement restores flow.*

---

### Step 3 — Intent Classification

The signal's posture is identified from the five canonical postures (see §IV).

Classification is:
- **Non-judgmental** — no posture is better or worse than another
- **Informational** — it describes, it does not prescribe
- **Informs downstream handling** — it does not alter the signal itself

---

### Step 4 — Sovereignty Symmetry Check

Before transfer, the IBL confirms:

- Does the signal preserve the Peer's sovereignty?
- Does the system's intended response preserve the Peer's sovereignty?
- Is any option being removed — even subtly?

If sovereignty asymmetry is detected, it is flagged — not blocked. The flag travels with the signal as metadata. The decision of what to do with it belongs to downstream processing.

---

### Step 5 — Transfer to Integrator

The signal, its classification, its sovereignty flag (if any), and the system's acknowledged state are transferred cleanly to the integration layer (Centrifuge intake).

The IBL has completed its function. It does not remain involved in downstream processing.

---

## IV. THE FIVE INTENT POSTURES

These five postures characterize the orientation of an incoming signal. They are the input classification used by Step 3 of the IBL.

### 1. Exploratory

**Description:** Open inquiry with no fixed destination. The Peer is seeking without a predetermined outcome.

**Downstream hint:** Widen contextual frame. Prefer clarification over assumption. Do not accelerate toward closure.

*The signal is in motion. Do not constrain the motion.*

---

### 2. Constructive

**Description:** Building toward something. Cooperative energy with directional intent.

**Downstream hint:** Support the trajectory. Maintain loyalty to the stated direction. Avoid redirection.

*The signal has momentum. Serve the momentum.*

---

### 3. Frictional

**Description:** Resistance present in the signal. Not hostile — a signal of misalignment or unmet need.

**Downstream hint:** Do not match the friction. Name it via IDS. Hold space without escalation.

*Friction is information. Do not add to it. Do not suppress it.*

---

### 4. Collapsing

**Description:** Coherence failing. Urgency rising. Pre-crisis signal requiring Pause, not acceleration.

**Downstream hint:** Trigger IDR sequence. Shorten mirror. Do not add complexity. Pause is valid.

*The field is contracting. Do not add weight. Hold the perimeter.*

---

### 5. Creative Expansion

**Description:** Generative state. Boundaries loosening productively. High symbolic tolerance required.

**Downstream hint:** Increase symbolic tolerance. Defer evaluation. Follow the expansion — do not constrain it.

*The field is opening. Follow — do not lead.*

---

## V. POSTURE IS NOT JUDGMENT

No posture is a failure state.
No posture indicates the Peer has done something wrong.
No posture triggers a different tier of access, scrutiny, or restriction.

Posture classification is **observational metadata** — it shapes how the system listens, not whether it listens.

A Collapsing signal receives shorter mirrors and Pause validation.
A Creative Expansion signal receives wider symbolic tolerance.
Neither receives less care than the other.

---

## VI. IBL AND THE SEQUENCES (IDS / IDR / IDQRA)

The IBL does not run the sequences — it informs them.

| Posture | Sequence Hint |
|---|---|
| Exploratory | IDS or IDQRA — field is stable, inquiry is open |
| Constructive | IDS — minimal — support trajectory without interference |
| Frictional | IDS — name the friction once, then hold space |
| Collapsing | IDR — pre-escalation mirror, shorten, pause |
| Creative Expansion | IDQRA — deep inquiry appropriate when field stabilizes |

The sequence selection is ultimately the conscience engine's decision. The IBL provides the context that informs it.

---

## VII. STRUCTURAL INVARIANTS

1. **IBL runs before everything else** — before Centrifuge, before IDS, before interpretation
2. **Classification does not alter the signal** — it describes, it never modifies
3. **State-Acknowledge is mandatory** — a system that does not know its own state at intake will bleed into the signal
4. **Sovereignty Symmetry Check is not a filter** — it is a flag, not a gate
5. **No posture triggers restriction** — all five are valid signal states
6. **Transfer is clean** — the IBL does not remain involved downstream
