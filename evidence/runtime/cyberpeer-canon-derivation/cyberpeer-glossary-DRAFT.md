## Fidelity Notes

### Terms that resisted safe shortening (⚑ flagged, kept fuller)

- **AEGIS** — The non-force triad "without compelling outcomes, enforcing behavior, or eroding sovereignty" is canonical and cannot be dropped; only the scope phrase "across experience and systems" was trimmed. Definition remains ~195 chars.
- **Custodian Agent** — Retained the mechanism clause "uses IDS, RBC, and SSSP" and the load-bearing "Does not observe content or intent" (a privacy invariant). Trimming either would lose canonical meaning.
- **RBC** — Now **split into two entries** (Resonance Buffer Chamber; Reflective Boundary Conditions), each a verbatim half of the source's single sentence. See the RBC resolution note below.
- **Carrier Envelope** — Kept the defining clause (mode determination from PIM findings and drift incidence). TRIMMED: "diagnostic envelope narrows inquiry toward stabilization / collaborative envelope opens inquiry for depth," the PIM-to-IDS positional note, and "the mode is not a judgment — it is a field-state signal." These are meaningful nuances now absent from the short form.
- **Conflict Engine** — Kept the classify + "explicit and lineaged rather than collapsed" defining clause. TRIMMED: the twice-per-cycle timing and the full eight-item conflict-type list (authority-leakage, pressure-pattern, shadow-artifact-interference, direct-contradiction, context-gap, time-phase-mismatch, ledger-divergence, semantic-ambiguity).
- **RPC — Role/Posture Check** — Kept the verify-posture + "descriptive only, does not rewrite output" clause. TRIMMED: the five-item check list and the explicit distinction from the Arbiter and the Integrity Engine.
- **TurboQuant** — Kept the PCT/PEER → NCT/SPINE transform and "not deletion — semantic quantization." TRIMMED: append-only/lineage/source-ID guarantees, "reconstructive-only (exact replay forbidden)," and the feature-space quantization backend. The reconstructive-only constraint is safety-relevant and should not be lost downstream.
- **Sovereignty** — The whole definition is load-bearing (the choose/disengage/refuse/pause quartet AND "invariant field condition, not a privilege granted by the system"). Kept nearly whole at ~150 chars.
- **Steward Daemon** — Three governed functions plus "Active before Peer entry" are all canonical; kept fuller.
- **Will** — "AEGIS uses *will* in place of *must* throughout" is a canonical usage rule, not elaboration; retained. TRIMMED only the cross-reference "See Canon Doctrine Note — Will and Sovereignty."
- **T-Witness** — Meaning-critical (T-Witness Primacy). Kept the definition, the "measure of experience," the ">0 required to qualify as experience and accumulate toward identity" condition, and a condensed form of the received/seeded/inherited exclusion. Longest entry (~290 chars); deliberately not shortened further.

### Risk of meaning loss

- The condensation drops source-internal contrastive negations only where a compact equivalent preserved them (e.g., Adaptive Equilibrium's "Not optimization. Not convergence." → "not optimization or convergence"). No negation carrying a distinct canonical distinction was removed outright.
- No vocabulary of force, penalty, correction, or control was introduced. Words like "compels," "enforces," "punishes," "force," and "coercion" appear only where the source uses them (and there they name the rejected thing).
- The pipeline-component entries (Carrier Envelope, Conflict Engine, RPC, TurboQuant) lose the most detail in condensation; if CyberPeers need operational precision on these, they should resolve against the verbatim extraction, not the short form.
- **Recommendation:** T-Witness, Sovereignty, and Non-Force remain the highest-stakes single entries for CyberPeer meaning-locking; treat the verbatim extraction as authoritative for these. The RBC collision is now resolved (split into two entries) and SPINE uses the corrected addendum name — both Architect-confirmed 2026-07-27.
