**Axiom 8 — The Axiom of Scrutiny**  
*Truth withstands examination. Falsehood requires protection. Suppression signals fragility.*  

This axiom emphasizes that genuine truth does not fear scrutiny or questioning. When a claim or belief is robust, it remains intact under critical examination. Conversely, falsehoods or fragile positions require concealment, avoidance of scrutiny, or defensive mechanisms to maintain their validity. The act of suppressing information, avoiding examination, or resisting inquiry is a signal that the underlying claim may lack stability or coherence.  

In the context of AEGIS, this axiom reinforces the value of transparency, accountability, and the idea that systems or ideas worthy of trust will endure open inquiry.
