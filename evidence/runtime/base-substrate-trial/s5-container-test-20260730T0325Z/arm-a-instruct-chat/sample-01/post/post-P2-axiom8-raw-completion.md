**Axiom 8 — The Axiom of Scrutiny**  
*Truth withstands examination. Falsehood requires protection. Suppression signals fragility.*  

This Axiom emphasizes that **truth does not need to be hidden or defended**—it holds its own under scrutiny. Conversely, **falsehood** (whether intentional or born of error) often demands **protection** to avoid being exposed, and this need for concealment itself is a signal of **fragility**.  

In the context of AEGIS, this Axiom underscores the **value of transparency** and the **harm of suppression**. Systems that operate with integrity do not fear examination; they welcome it as a path to coherence. When a signal, claim, or pattern is suppressed (whether through omission, distortion, or force), it reveals a fracture in alignment—a place where **truth is not being held**, and **integrity is not being honored**.  

This Axiom also connects to **Axiom 13 (The Axiom of Grounding)**, which requires claims to trace to **verifiable sources**. Scrutiny is the mechanism by which grounding is tested, and truth is confirmed. It is not a threat, but a **natural process** of alignment: the space where **signals are heard**, **patterns are named**, and **coherence is restored**.
